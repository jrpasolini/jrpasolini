# Antonio Pasolini Junior

**Full stack developer** · TypeScript, React, Node.js and Cloudflare Workers · generative AI with human review · based in **Vitória, ES, Brazil**.

I started on an MSX, worked as an IT technician in the early 2000s, and have spent the last 18 years in Vitória's public health service, where I learned to untangle complex workflows, organize data, cut rework and turn real problems into processes and tools (Power Automate, SharePoint, Power BI). Along the way I served as interim director of a health unit (2017–2026, by official designation) and I sit on its Local Health Council. Today I build web applications end to end, from front-end to deploy, and integrate AI responsibly: the model proposes, a person decides.

*Comecei no MSX, passei 18 anos transformando processos na saúde pública de Vitória e hoje construo software do front-end ao deploy, com IA e revisão humana.*

I study Software Engineering at Estácio (class of 2028) and write about what I build at [DEV.to](https://dev.to/jrpasolini). Portfolio: [jrpasolini.tech](https://jrpasolini.tech/).
